Here’s a clear, concise breakdown of what the **Auto-Split Hook** project does and why it’s valuable:

---

### **Auto-Split Hook: One-Sentence Pitch**  
*A Uniswap v4 hook + gasless mini-app that **automatically splits LP fees or swap revenue** across teams, DAOs, or contributors—with optional sponsored transactions for seamless onboarding.*

---

### **How It Works (User Flows)**  
#### **1. For Liquidity Providers (LPs) / Pool Creators**  
- **Problem:** LPs earn fees, but manually splitting them (e.g., 70% to team, 20% to DAO, 10% to charity) is cumbersome.  
- **Solution:**  
  - When creating a Uniswap v4 pool, attach the **Auto-Split Hook**.  
  - Set predefined split percentages (e.g., `[0xTeam: 70%, 0xDAO: 20%, 0xCharity: 10%]`).  
  - **Result:** Every time swap fees accumulate, the hook **auto-splits and distributes** them to the designated wallets.  

#### **2. For Project Teams / DAOs**  
- **Problem:** Revenue from NFTs, donations, or other apps needs manual splitting.  
- **Solution:**  
  - Use the **standalone Splitter.sol** (same contract powering the hook).  
  - Deploy a splitter via the **Base mini-app** (e.g., for NFT mint revenue).  
  - Enable **gasless mode** so users don’t pay fees (sponsor covers gas).  
  - **Result:** Funds sent to the splitter are auto-distributed, with no extra work.  

#### **3. For End Users**  
- **Problem:** New users struggle with gas fees.  
- **Solution:**  
  - Interact with apps using the splitter **without ETH** (thanks to gasless relayer).  
  - Example: Mint an NFT → team gets paid → user pays $0 in gas.  

---

### **Key Components**  
1. **Uniswap v4 Hook**  
   - Listens for swap/LP fee events.  
   - Diverts a % of fees to the `Splitter.sol` contract.  
   - Optional: Let LPs **vote on split ratios** (governance).  

2. **Splitter.sol (Core Smart Contract)**  
   - Accepts ETH/tokens and splits them based on preset ratios.  
   - Reusable for **NFTs, donations, DeFi protocols**.  

3. **Gasless Mini-App (Frontend)**  
   - Deploy splitters in 1 click (Base-compatible).  
   - Toggle gasless mode (sponsors pay fees).  
   - **Splits Directory** (showcases projects using it → drives engagement).  

---

### **Why It’s Valuable**  
- **For Uniswap:** Introduces **self-managing LP pools** (no manual splits).  
- **For Base:** Drives **onchain activity** (teams deploying splitters = more txs).  
- **For BuidlGuidl:** Teaches **hook development + gasless patterns**.  
- **For Users:** Simplifies revenue sharing **without coding or manual work**.  

---

### **Real-World Example**  
A DAO creates a Uniswap v4 pool with the hook:  
- **80% of fees** go to the DAO treasury.  
- **20%** go to a developer grant fund.  
→ Fees **auto-split on every swap**, with no extra transactions.  

Meanwhile, the same team uses the **standalone splitter** for their NFT mint revenue, also split 80/20.  

---

It’s a **"set-and-forget" revenue splitter** for:  
1. **Uniswap LPs** (auto-split fees via hook).  
2. **Any onchain project** (NFTs, donations, etc.).  
3. **Gasless users** (sponsors cover fees).  

All built on one codebase to save time. 🚀
